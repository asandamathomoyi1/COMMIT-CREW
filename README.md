# COMMIT-CREW
Overview:
MedTrack helps students with chronic medical conditions like diabetes, asthma, epilepsy and others to stay on track with their medication schedule and to quickly find a nearby open pharmacy in times where they need help or run low on medicine.
Problem Statement:
Students with chronic illnesses juggle irregular timetables, a social life and independent living. Missed doses are extremely common, not because of forgetfulness, but by unpredictable student lifestyles. Most reminder apps are designed for general tasks rather than time critical medical needs. Many apps depend on internet connection and push notifications that may fail when needed most. This is why we built MedWise to give students a reliable medication management app.
